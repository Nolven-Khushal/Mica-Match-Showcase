# Mica Match: every feature, screen by screen

Mica Match is a photo-to-shade matching tool for laminate sales. A rep photographs a sample, and the app returns the closest shades from the catalogue with design numbers. Everything below is shown with a synthetic demo catalogue; no real product data is used.

The app is phone-first. On a desktop it appears as a phone-shaped column in the middle of the window.

---

## 1. Sign in

- The brand logo, a small line with the brand name, and the "Mica Match" title.
- An animated globe with glowing dots for the cities where the team works.
- **Your name** and **Team access code** fields, and a **Get started** button. The enter key submits.
- Friendly errors: wrong code, "too many tries, wait a minute" (sign-in is rate limited), and "could not reach the server".
- The name is remembered on the device and greets the rep on the home screen.

## 2. Entrance fly-through

- A short cinematic fly-through of the catalogue covers ("The Library") plays after the first sign-in of each day.
- A **Skip** button ends it at any time.
- It can be replayed from Settings.

## 3. Library (home)

- A greeting that follows the time of day, the date, and the rep's name.
- **One search box** for shade name, design number, finish, colour word or catalogue.
- **Thickness chips** (All, 0.7 mm, 0.8 mm, 1 mm, 1.25 mm) that narrow the stack to the catalogues of that thickness.
- **The catalogue stack:** each catalogue is a card with its cover, thickness badge, name and shade count. Swipe or drag to browse, tap a side card to bring it forward, with spring motion and a gentle fade of the cards that are further away.
- A row under the stack with the catalogue's name, thickness, shade count and an **Open** button.
- **A fan of sample shades** from the selected catalogue, and an **On the wall** grid of tiles, each with the shade name and design number.
- A "Which shade were you matching?" banner appears when the rep is teaching the app (see section 13).

## 4. Search results

- The result count, with the scope of the search.
- A "did you mean" suggestion for near-miss spellings.
- Catalogue chips for the catalogues that contain the words typed.
- A grid of matching tiles. An empty state explains how to search when nothing matches.

## 5. The pull-out stage

- **Open** lifts the catalogue off the stack, lays it on a table, opens it, and fans its shades out.
- The catalogue's title, thickness and count appear, with a button to open the whole catalogue and a back button.
- Tapping any shade in the fan opens it large.

## 6. Catalogue page

- The cover shown as a small 3D book, the thickness, and the number of shades.
- A search field inside the catalogue: digits search by design number, words search by name.
- A grid of every shade in the catalogue that loads more as the rep scrolls, with a running count.

## 7. Shade viewer

- The shade picture shown large, with its design number and finish, catalogue, thickness and name.
- **View settings:** brightness, contrast, colour strength and warmth sliders, to preview the shade under different light.
- When opened from a scan: a match percentage, and a switch between **Swatch**, **Split** (the customer's photo and the catalogue shade side by side) and **Your photo**.
- **Share:** sends the shade picture through the phone's share sheet, or saves it on a computer.
- **Add to board** (or the name of the board it is already on).
- **Enquire on WhatsApp** (section 8).
- When opened from a scan: **This is the right shade** and **Not it** (section 13).

## 8. Enquiry

- A sheet that asks for name, city, number of sheets (8 x 4 ft, with plus and minus buttons), where it will be used (kitchen, wardrobe, furniture, wall cladding, commercial project, not sure yet) and an optional note.
- It writes the message for the rep, including the shade, catalogue and thickness, and opens WhatsApp to the sales desk's number.
- The rep's name and city are remembered for next time.

## 9. Scan: the camera

- A live rear-camera view with a thirds grid and a "Fill the frame" guide.
- **Light check** (too dark, too bright, light good) and **glare check** ("tilt the phone") that update live.
- Tap to focus, with a focus ring.
- A shutter button with a progress ring for up to **three shots**; thumbnails of the shots so far with a counter; a flash effect and a small vibration on each shot.
- **Use N shots** when at least one is taken. Averaging several shots cancels glare and colour drift.
- A gallery button to pick an existing photo instead.
- If the camera is blocked or missing, a friendly message and a **Take a photo** button that uses the phone's own camera app.

## 10. Crop

- For photos picked from the gallery: the photo on a stage with a square frame.
- Drag to move, pinch or use the zoom slider to zoom, then **Next**. The cropped photo joins the shots.

## 11. Adjust ("match the real thing")

- Step 2 of 3. The photo is shown large, with the hint to hold the phone next to the real sheet and adjust until they look the same.
- **Brightness, contrast, colour strength and warmth** sliders with live preview.
- **Remove glare** (with undo), **Auto** (a careful automatic correction), and **Reset**.
- Hints when glare is spotted, or when there is so much that a retake is better.
- **Find matches**.

## 12. Loading

- Step 3 of 3, with a shimmering placeholder wall while the matching runs.

## 13. Results and the learning loop

- "15 closest matches" with a thumbnail of the photo that was taken.
- A fan of the best nine, a **Design / Colour / Both** switch that re-orders the shortlist, and a grid of tiles, each with a match percentage.
- Tapping a tile opens the shade viewer, where the rep confirms with **This is the right shade** or says **Not it**. A confirmation saves the photo and the true shade for retraining and shows a short thank-you.
- **None of these, pick the right shade:** takes the rep to the library with a banner "Which shade were you matching?". They find the shade, tap it, confirm in a dialog, and the correction is saved with the photo.
- **Try another photo** starts again.
- An empty state with tips when nothing is found.

## 14. Colours

- **Build a colour:** a rainbow bar and sliders for colour, richness and lightness, with a live swatch and a plain-words description such as "light grey-blue".
- **From a photo:** pick a photo, tap the colour you want on it.
- **Solid colours only** switch.
- "Closest shades" with a label for each: *Very close*, *Close* or *Visible difference*.

## 15. Boards

- One board per customer or job. A list with the board name, number of shades, date and small round previews.
- **New board** asks for a name.
- **Board page:** a fan and a grid of the pinned shades, the customer's photo (add or replace), a notes field, **Share board** and delete.
- **Share board** makes one clean image sheet with the board name, photo, shade grid with design numbers and names, and the note. It is shared like any picture.
- Shades can be pinned from the viewer, from anywhere in the app, and removed again.

## 16. More

- **History:** every match, correction and enquiry on this device, with date and outcome.
- **Team:** a globe, a leaderboard of who is teaching the app and how often the right shade was in the top 15, and an overall accuracy bar.
- **What's new:** recently added catalogues, each opening its catalogue.
- **Settings** (next section).

## 17. Settings

- Name, and the WhatsApp number that enquiries go to.
- **Appearance:** Auto, Dark or Light.
- How to install on a phone (Add to Home Screen).
- Replay the entrance animation. Sign out.

## 18. The rotary menu

- A round knob at the bottom right. Tap it, then tap an icon; or press, slide to an icon and let go.
- Library, Scan, Colours, Boards, More, and Close. The current section is highlighted, the knob's caption names the icon under the finger, and the phone gives a small vibration.

## 19. Everywhere

- **Light and dark themes**, following the phone or chosen by hand, with one consistent lighting model across every screen.
- Installable to the home screen; works in iPhone and Android browsers with no store install.
- Smooth page transitions that respect the phone's back button.
- Boards, history, name and settings are stored on the device.

---

## What is behind the screens (not visible in the app)

- **Matching engine:** a self-supervised vision model plus a small head trained on simulated bad phone photos, a colour re-rank that corrects phone colour bias, and an averaging step for multi-shot capture.
- **Learning loop:** corrections and confirmations are stored with their photos and used to retrain the head; a new head is kept only if it beats the old one on photos it never saw.
- **Offline engine:** the whole matching engine also runs inside the Android app, so it works with no network (about half a second per photo).
- **Catalogue pipeline:** catalogue PDFs are turned into a shade library by reading each page, cross-checking every design number against a product master, and storing which catalogues each shade belongs to.
- **Access and safety:** a shared team code, rate-limited sign-in, upload size limits, and no catalogue data in the code.
- **Delivery:** a web site, an installable home-screen app, and an Android app; the store release needs a closed test with 12 testers for 14 days.
